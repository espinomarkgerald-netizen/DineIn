SYSTEMS IMPLEMENTED
- Fine Dining demand grows with progression, usable table capacity, staff service capacity,
  remaining evening time, accepted bookings and estimated meal/drink workload.
- Reservations dominate daily demand; walk-ins are limited to one or two guests.
  Arrival spacing and table occupancy windows prevent overlapping new bookings.
  Existing accepted reservation identities, table assignments and times are retained.
- Reserved parties have no reception patience countdown. Fine Dining walk-ins drain
  reception patience at half the ordinary rate. Earlier restaurants retain their behavior.
- Existing reception chairs are claimed, approached and occupied using customer seating
  animation. Larger parties can use distinct authored standing positions. Chairs release
  when escorting begins; guests begin following the receptionist before table arrival.
- One compact reservation marker per party; details show on selection/handling.
- Fine Dining eating is 1.5 times ordinary eating; other task durations are unchanged.
- Optional once-per-meal drink requests reuse the notepad, menu, inventory, kitchen,
  bar output slots, pickup/delivery and final bill. Only served drinks add a bill line.
  Cancelled, unavailable or failed optional service restores the original meal identity.
- Wine refill equips a temporary bottle, preserves carried items, opens the existing-style
  animated blue UI, supports mouse/touch drag and fill, then completes or returns for retry.
- Finished transparent wine sprites, stream/splash feedback, success pop and existing audio.
  Wine glasses use the tray's existing serving placement and cleanup flow.

DEFAULT BALANCE / INSPECTOR SETTINGS
FineDiningReservations on the Lobby3 scene:
- Early 6-8, mid 9-14, late 15-20 groups; 20 is a cap for new demand, not a quota.
- Mid tier starts at day 6 with at least 4 usable tables; late at day 15 with at least 7.
- Reservation share 85%; new requests available through 8 PM.
- Minimum arrival spacing 10 in-game minutes; large-party spacing 20 minutes.
- Expected capacity occupation 85 in-game minutes including extra eating/drink/cleanup work.
- Reserved reception patience disabled; walk-in patience multiplier 2.
- Eating multiplier 1.5; extra-drink event chance 50%, trigger after 35% of meal duration,
  optional request window 75 seconds. Maximum one event per group.
- Refill choice chance 50% when eligible; currently Red Wine must already be in the meal,
  unlocked, enabled on the menu and in stock. Other eligible events request an extra drink.
- Editable recipe list, bottle/indicator prefabs, hand offset/rotation/scale.
FineDiningWaitingArea: existing 8 chair bindings, 10 distinct standing anchors, following spacing.
KitchenManager: 2 editable bar tray slots, bar pickup approach and bar-only tray scale 0.55.
WinePouringUI prefab: sprites, stage, pointer capture radius, bottle tilt, smoothing,
initial/target fill, timing and completion feedback. Defaults include 2.4-second pouring,
78% target fill, 65-degree tilt and approximately 0.15-0.3-second feedback transitions.

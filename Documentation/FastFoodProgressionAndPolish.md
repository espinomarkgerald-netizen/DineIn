# Fast Food progression and polish

Unlocks make equipment available to purchase; they do not grant it free.
Existing saved unlocks and purchases are retained. Use a fresh campaign to
verify the starting experience.

| Day | New availability |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Five usable seats, one counter, burger, fries, existing drinks, takeout |
| 2 | First extra stool |
| 3 | Chicken nuggets; seating expansions begin |
| 4 | Kiosk 1 |
| 5 | Chicken sandwich; pink diners |
| 6 | Second cashier station and second cashier hiring/assignment |
| 7 | Fried chicken |
| 8 | Card payments |
| 9 | Busser trolley |
| 10 | Fish fillet sandwich; blue diners |
| 11 | Kiosk 2 |
| 14 | Waiter trolley and optional delivery assistant |

Additional seating purchases unlock progressively through day 26.
Locked table and service-station roots are disabled until purchased. The existing
restaurant coordinator restores them on purchase/save-load events; starter
seating stays active. Unlock notifications announce purchase availability.
Pink diners still request table delivery; takeout customers collect their bags.

## Tuning and files

- Assets/_Project/Resources/FastFood/Progression.asset: traffic, patience,
  quota curves, customer introduction days, takeout/delivery proportions and
  equipment catalog. Curve X is the actual day; difficulty plateaus at day 15.
- Equipment assets under Assets/_Project/Resources/FastFood/: prices and days.
- Assets/_Project/Resources/MenuCatalog.asset: Fast Food recipes and ingredients.
  Recipe assets hold price, unlock day, ingredients, icon and serving prefab.
- Assets/_Project/Restaurant/Prefabs/FastFood/Food/: normalized serving models.
  Sources: Assets/_Project/UI/Assets/FoodIcons/Fast Food/Models/.
- Assets/_Project/Scenes/RoleBased/Lobby2.unity: table/station upgrade references
  and second cashier bindings on FastFoodLobbyAuthoring.
- Assets/_Project/Resources/UI/RestaurantBossTips.prefab: coaching text, delay,
  daily limit and existing Big Boss portrait/dialogue presentation.

## Integration

FastFoodProgressionSettings extends existing equipment, staffing, recipe,
save and difficulty systems. FastFoodTable/Booth enforce usable capacity;
FastFoodServiceStation gates access. EmployeeManager and LobbyAutonomousService
support two independently working cashiers after purchase.

UnlockCelebrationManager detects new days without scene reloads and waits for
save loading. Notifications include the day. LobbyHUDRedesign highlights unread
newspapers with a NEW badge, warm tint and pulse; reading clears the reminder.
Reduced-motion disables pulsing. DailyNewspaperPresenter covers the legacy UI.
The unlock popup reserves separate title, description, day/location and button
bands, with bounded text sizing. Fast Food ingredient ItemData sprites point to
the supplied Ingredients folder; cheese and drinks keep their existing art where
no matching replacement was supplied.
RestaurantBossTips shows non-blocking quiet-service tips, avoids modal/tutorial
interruptions, and saves which tips have appeared.

## Verification

Passed: Unity compilation; authoring validation of six modeled foods, unique
ingredient/equipment IDs, five starting seats, one starting station, second
cashier references, difficulty curves and Boss UI. All six model bounds have a
grounded base and normalized footprint. C# whitespace checks passed; the full
diff reports trailing spaces generated by Unity's scene serializer in Lobby2.

Not run: play-mode playthrough, visual UI acceptance, economy/rush balancing,
or multiplayer regression testing. Player saves were not reset for testing.

Follow-up checks passed: isolated edit-mode locked/purchased/reset visibility
transitions, starter-seat visibility, and no text overflow or vertical band
overlap for all 22 equipment unlock messages. Live play-mode visual checks remain.

## Manual acceptance checklist

1. Fresh Fast Food run: five assignable seats, one counter, two foods plus drinks.
2. Advance days: notifications appear once, including without scene reloads;
   reload saves and confirm seen notifications do not repeat.
3. Read newspaper: reminder clears; next unread issue restores it. Check both levels.
4. Verify locked kiosks/seats/cashier cannot be used before purchase; buy upgrades
   after unlock and check assignment/service.
5. Hire two cashiers: check independent work, resting/reassignment, wages and reload.
6. Check dishes on trays/tables, unchanged drinks, takeout pickup and pink delivery.
7. Play early/middle/late days; tune revenue, costs, queue time and workload curves.
8. Check readable quiet-service Boss tips without input blocking; menus, hygiene
   decisions and tutorial dialogue must suppress them.

Editor menu: Dine In > Fast Food > Validate Progression runs asset checks.
Install is an explicit authoring/reset-to-defaults utility, not a runtime step;
do not rerun over custom tuning without reviewing its defaults.
